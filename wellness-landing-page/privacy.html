Privacy Policy

Last updated: 05/11/2026

This policy explains how Bailey ("we", "me") collects and uses your personal information when you use this website, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). I am an independent Brand Partner, and this website is an independent distributor site.

1. Who is responsible

Responsible party: Bailey, Gauteng, South Africa. Contact: Bailey@gmail.com, +27 83 268 7456.

2. What we collect

  • The details you enter in the form: your full name, WhatsApp number and email address.
  • Which advert or link brought you here (such as UTM campaign parameters and Meta’s ad click identifier, fbclid), plus the Meta Pixel browser identifiers (the _fbc and _fbp cookies). These are saved with your enquiry so we can tell which adverts work.
  • Technical data such as browser type and pages visited, collected through cookies and the Meta Pixel.

3. Why we collect it

  • To contact you via WhatsApp, SMS, phone or email about the wellness products and business information you asked about.
  • To answer your questions and arrange a free introductory chat.
  • To measure and improve the performance of our advertising.

We only process your information because you gave us your consent by submitting the form and ticking the consent box. You may withdraw that consent at any time.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. It is processed by service providers we use to run this website: Supabase (secure database where enquiries are stored), Make.com (automation), Telegram (a new-enquiry alert to me that contains only your name and what you’re interested in, never your phone number or email), Meta (Facebook and Instagram advertising, the Meta Pixel, and WhatsApp for our chat), and Vercel (website hosting). Some of these providers store data outside South Africa, including in the EU and the USA. We use providers that apply appropriate data protection safeguards.

If you become a customer or team member, relevant details may also be shared with the relevant product company as needed to process your order or registration.

5. Cookies and the Meta Pixel

This site uses the Meta Pixel, which sets cookies to measure ad performance and show relevant adverts. We also keep the advert you arrived from in your browser’s session storage until you close the tab, and, if sending the form fails, a copy of what you entered in your browser for up to 7 days so it can be re-sent automatically. You can manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings, or block cookies in your browser.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for 12 months from the date you submit the form. After that they are deleted automatically, unless you have become a customer. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time. Customer records are kept as long as needed for the relationship and legal requirements.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your information, including secure (HTTPS) connections and access-restricted systems. No online system is completely risk-free.

8. Your rights

You may ask us to confirm what information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop contacting you. To opt out of messages, reply "STOP" or email us. We will act on requests promptly. If you are unhappy with how your information is handled, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

This website uses the Meta Pixel, a tool provided by Meta Platforms, to measure how our advertising performs and to show our ads to relevant audiences. When you visit this site or submit the form, Meta may collect information such as your device, browser and the pages you view, and whether you completed the form. This information is handled under Meta’s own privacy terms. You can control how Meta uses your data through your Facebook ad settings (Settings → Ad preferences), or by using your browser’s tracking-protection or ad-blocking tools.
